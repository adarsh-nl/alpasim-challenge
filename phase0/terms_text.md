3. The Challenge
----------------

The Challenge is a two-track competition that allows contestants to submit end-to-end driving policies via Docker images that are simulated in AlpaSim on private datasets to generate results that are analyzed and compared against other teams’ Submissions. The nuPlan track restricts teams to use any publicly available data to develop their policies whereas the PAI track allows competitors to use any legally available data to develop their policies. Further descriptions and evaluation software for the Challenge can be found on the Challenge page and at https://github.com/NVlabs/alpasim/tree/e2e_challenge/e2e_challenge.

The dataset to be used in this Challenge is described on the Challenge Page. NVIDIA is not releasing a dataset in connection with this Challenge and participants are responsible for  complying with any applicable terms of datasets used.
